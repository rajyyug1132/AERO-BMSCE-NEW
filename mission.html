01 — Mission

We don't buy our aircraft.
We build them.

AeroBMSCE is the aeronautical society of BMS College of Engineering. Since 2010 we have designed, built and flown fixed-wing, VTOL and hybrid UAVs — indigenous airframes with integrated flight systems — and carried them to podiums from IIT Bombay to SAE Aero Design in Fort Worth, Texas.

Discipline

Systems integration over off-the-shelf assembly — every airframe and subsystem is engineered, not sourced.

Cadence

A continuous design–build–fly loop across national and international competition seasons.

People

Student engineers learning at the edge — the talent India's UAV industry hires from next.

See what this discipline produces.

Indigenous flight controllers, custom ESCs, and GPS-denied navigation — built in-house.
