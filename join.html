Process

Four steps to the flight line.

01

Apply

Send the form below. Tell us what you've built before — a robot, a website, a birdhouse. Anything counts.

02

Workshop

A weekend at Propel Lab-3 building a chuck glider. This is where you find out whether you like it.

03

Interview

Fifteen minutes with the core team. Not a viva — we're checking how you think, not what you've memorised.

04

Induction

You're assigned a division and a mentor, and you start on a live subsystem within the first month.
